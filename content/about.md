I am Darshan Vichhi, a Rust systems and AI engineer and the founder of
AarambhDevHub. I work mainly on software where architecture matters: AI
infrastructure, local-first learning, backend services, query execution,
security, graphics, animation, and developer tooling.

My approach is to build small but complete systems, test the claim at the centre
of each project, and document the trade-offs honestly. I care about readable
source, reproducible examples, clear maturity labels, and explaining where an
experiment ends and production engineering begins.
